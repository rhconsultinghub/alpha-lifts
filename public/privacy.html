Alpha Lifts — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Alpha Lifts is a workout tracking app. This policy describes what data the app handles, where it goes, and your choices. The short version: your training data lives on your device, syncs to our server only when you sign in, and is never sold or shared with advertisers.

Data stored on your device

Your workout programs, logged sessions, body weight and measurements, nutrition check-ins, settings, and progress photos are stored locally on your device. Progress photos never leave your device — they are not included in backups or cloud sync.

Account data and cloud sync

Creating an account requires an email address and password. Passwords are stored only as salted cryptographic hashes. When you are signed in, your training data (programs, workout history, settings — not photos) syncs to our server, hosted on Cloudflare infrastructure, so it follows you across devices. Deleting the app's data from Settings ("Reset App") also erases the synced cloud copy.

AI Coach

If you use the AI Coach, a summary of your training context (your program, recent sessions, aggregate stats, and — if you log them — body weight and nutrition summaries) is sent with your messages to our server, which forwards them to Anthropic's Claude API to generate responses. This data is used only to answer you; conversations are stored in your own app data, not used to train models.

Email

We send transactional email only (account verification and password reset), delivered via Resend. No marketing email, ever.

Notifications

Rest-timer alerts and workout reminders are generated on your device. If you enable cloud reminders on the web app, a push subscription for your browser is stored server-side solely to deliver those reminders.

Identifiers and analytics

The app generates a random device identifier used only to meter AI Coach usage. There are no third-party analytics, ad networks, or trackers of any kind.

Data deletion

You can erase all local and synced data at any time via Settings → Reset App. To delete your account entirely (including your email address), contact ryanhouse19@gmail.com and it will be removed promptly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it.
